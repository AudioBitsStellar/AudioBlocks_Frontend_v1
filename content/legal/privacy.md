Draft — pending legal review. This page is a placeholder so the Privacy Policy link is not empty. It is not a finished legal commitment.

## Data collected

AudioBlocks may process account details you submit, listening history in the app, and messages you send to support. This draft does not list every field or promise a retention period.

## Analytics and consent

Analytics, including Segment, stays off until you accept it in the consent banner. You can change that choice later in privacy settings. Rejecting analytics does not block playback.

## Wallet data and on-chain visibility

A connected wallet address can be stored with your account. Transactions you sign are public on the Stellar network. AudioBlocks cannot hide data that the network itself publishes.

## Cookies

The site can store a cookie or similar browser value for your analytics choice and for keeping you signed in. This draft does not name every cookie.

## Contact

Questions about this draft can be sent through the contact options published on the AudioBlocks site. A dedicated privacy mailbox has not been assigned in this draft.
